Autism in California: Start Here

You do not need a diagnosis to start. You do not need a doctor's referral. You do not need to wait for anything.

Five things for families, depending on where you are today, and two for people who work inside the system. None of them asks you for anything, and none of them is trying to sell you a service.

What these are, and what they are not

They are free, and there is nothing to sign up for. No account, no email address, no service being sold at the end.

Everything here comes from California's own published rules and files — the Department of Developmental Services, the Education Code, and the vendor lists the regional centres are required to publish. Each page says at the bottom exactly which source each deadline came from, so you can check any of it.

This is information, not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a person. Your regional centre's service coordinator, your local Family Resource Center — free, and staffed by parents of children with disabilities — and Disability Rights California can all tell you how any of this applies to your particular child. Call them. These pages exist to make that call shorter, not to replace it.

Written in California, September 2026. If a deadline here is wrong, it matters — the sources are listed on every page so a mistake can be found and corrected.
